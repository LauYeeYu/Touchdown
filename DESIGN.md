# Design System: Touchdown

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Written Logbook"**

Each view states its main point as one plain sentence with the figures inside it, then shows one subject at full size underneath: the period's map on Home, the period's routes on Flights, the route on a flight. Supporting context (most recent, aircraft cards, month by month, the flight list) sits below in quiet sections divided by hairline rules. The world comes from the owner's own lab site and photo gallery: warm paper ground, one sans family, a narrow reading measure, hairlines instead of boxes, and rounded photo cards with a tinted caption block.

Colour is reserved for data. Flown is blue, spotted is orange, and everything else is paper and ink. The page is flat and calm; depth appears only on things that float above it (menus, tooltips, toasts) and on a card under the pointer. Density is moderate: generous vertical spacing between sections, compact rows inside lists, and tabular figures wherever numbers line up.

The system refuses the KPI-tile grid and the flight-tracker wall of panels. A statistic belongs in a sentence or a chart with its own counted-what subtitle, not in a box of its own.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm paper ground in light, warm near-black in dark; both follow the device by default.
- One system sans for every role; display weight 650 with tight negative tracking.
- Two data inks, flown blue and spotted orange, and no other decorative colour.
- Lead sentences with phrases underlined in their data ink that point at the map or the aircraft.
- Hairline rules and tonal paper bands instead of borders and boxes.
- Credited photo cards with 12px corners and a tinted caption block.
- Every precise time shown with its date and UTC offset.

## Colors

Paper and ink with two data inks; neutrals are warm (hue around 85-95 in OKLCH) in both themes.

### Primary
- **Flown Blue** (light `flown`, dark `flown-dark`): everything about flights flown. Great-circle arcs, recorded tracks, route arrows, flown dots, the flights bar chart, the cumulative line and its wash. Also the interface's single interactive colour: focus rings, text caret, selection wash, input focus border, checkbox accent, and the current tab on the phone tab bar (in its text-safe `flown-ink` shade).
- **Flown Text Blue** (`flown-ink`, `flown-ink-dark`): the shade of flown blue used when blue has to be read as text.

### Secondary
- **Spotted Orange** (light `spotted`, dark `spotted-dark`): everything about aircraft photographed. Spotting dots on maps, spotted dots in cards and legends, the new-aircraft bar chart.
- **Spotted Text Orange** (`spotted-ink`, `spotted-ink-dark`): spotted orange as readable text, for example the "new to your log" count.

### Tertiary
- **Check Green** (`good`, `good-dark`): the icon of a passed-checks status line only. Never a fill, never a chart colour.

### Neutral
- **Warm Paper** (`paper`): the page ground in light theme. Fixed by the brand; plain white is a fallback, not a choice.
- **Paper Band** (`paper-band`): the header band, the phone tab bar, card caption blocks, and the hovered or active flight row. Also the browser theme colour.
- **Deep Paper** (`paper-deep`): the selected segment and the photo placeholder.
- **Field** (`field`): text inputs and selects, a touch lighter than the page.
- **Hairline** (`rule`): section rules, row dividers, the times strip, the header's bottom edge.
- **Strong Hairline** (`rule-strong`) and **Hover Hairline** (`rule-hover`): outlines of controls (segmented control, quiet buttons, inputs, chips) at rest and on hover.
- **Ink** (`ink`), **Ink 2** (`ink-2`), **Ink 3** (`ink-3`): primary text; secondary text and inactive controls; labels, captions, credits, and axis text.
- **Map neutrals** (`land`, `land-edge`, `sphere`, `chart-grid`): land fill, coastline stroke in the paper colour, the globe disc on phones, and chart gridlines.
- **Washes** (`hover-wash`, `selected-wash`): ink at 5% and 7% for hover and current-page states.

### Named Rules
**The Two Inks Rule.** Flown blue and spotted orange are the only data colours. A chart, map layer, dot or underline about flights is blue; about spotting is orange; about both is split half blue, half orange. No third data colour is introduced; new series are separated by position, dash or label.

**The Validated Pair Rule.** Each data ink has a light and a dark value that were checked against their own ground (`flown` and `spotted` on `paper`; `flown-dark` and `spotted-dark` on `paper-dark`). Use the pair together; do not lift one theme's ink into the other.

**The Word With The Icon Rule.** Status is never colour alone. A status line pairs an icon with a written word or sentence ("Needs a look.", "All checks passed: ..."); only the passed icon carries `good`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** the system sans stack (ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, SF Pro Text, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** the same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** the same stack with tabular figures; there is no monospace face

**Character:** One familiar sans, set with care: heavy and tightly tracked for codes and titles, regular weight for the sentences the owner reads. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from a second family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (650, 5rem, 0.9, -0.035em): the airport codes on a flight's route hero. 3.25rem on phones.
- **Headline** (400, 2rem, 1.32, -0.02em, max 31em, balanced wrapping): the lead sentence on Home and Flights. Figures inside it step up to 650 with tabular figures. 1.5rem on phones.
- **Headline, flight** (400, 1.5rem, max 34em): the lead sentence under a flight's date line. 1.25rem on phones.
- **Route** (600, 2.25rem, 1, -0.02em): route codes in the most-recent block; 1.875rem on phones. Session titles use the same voice at 1.625rem.
- **Clock** (600, 1.75rem, 1.1, tabular): times in the times strip; 1.5rem on phones.
- **Title** (650, 1.375rem, 1.25, -0.015em): section titles. 1.25rem on phones. Month headings in the list use the smaller title (650, 1.125rem, -0.01em).
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5): running text and definition values. Most interface text sits one step down at 0.9375rem (nav, buttons, row cells, menu items).
- **Stat** (650, 1.25rem, 1.25, -0.015em, tabular): values in the stats under the lead sentence; units inside them at 0.72em, weight 500, in `ink-2`. 1.125rem on phones.
- **Label** (400, 0.8125rem): column heads, time labels, definition terms, legends, tooltips, footer. Credits drop to 0.75rem (0.6875rem under thumbnails).
- **Chart label** (11px, tabular): axis ticks and bar values (bar values at 600).
- **Wordmark** (700, 0.9375rem, 0.16em, capitals): the name in the header only.

### Named Rules
**The One Family Rule.** Sans-serif only, from the system stack. No serif, no webfont, no monospace. Contrast comes from weight (400, 500, 600, 650, 700) and size.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Any number that sits in a column, a strip, a chart or a lead-sentence figure uses tabular figures, so values align and do not shift as they change.

**The Dated Time Rule.** Every precise time is shown with its date and its UTC offset (for example "Tue 4 Mar · UTC+8"), in 24-hour local time of the airport, with a "+1 day" chip when the landing date differs. A clock time never appears alone.

## Layout

A single centred column: `min(1120px, 100% - 2 × gutter)`, gutter 32px, 16px on phones. Views open 40px below the header (24px on phones) with the period control, then the lead sentence 24px below, then the subject at full column width. Sections are separated by 64px (44px on phones), each with a title row (title left, aside or link right, baseline-aligned) 20px above its content. Two-column splits use 7:5 or 5:7 with a 48px gap; chart pairs use two equal columns with a 48px gap. The main area keeps 96px of bottom padding (120px on phones, to clear the tab bar).

Spacing runs on 4px steps, mainly 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48 and 64px.

**Responsive rules.**
- **Up to 960px:** the flight list drops the aircraft and distance columns.
- **Up to 720px:** the gutter becomes 16px; the header shrinks to 56px with wordmark, sync icon (text hidden), theme and Add; navigation moves to a fixed bottom tab bar; split and chart grids stack to one column with a 36px gap; the gallery becomes a horizontal scroll-snap strip with cards at 78% width; list rows become a two-line layout (route and duration on top, date and meta below); the times strip becomes two by two; breadcrumbs lose the pager.
- **Maps under 600px wide:** the Flights and flight maps become a disc centred on the period (an orthographic globe when everything fits on one hemisphere, an azimuthal equal-area disc otherwise) about as tall as it is wide, rather than a shallow world strip. Home keeps the full-width world cropped to the period's latitudes.

### Named Rules
**The One Subject Rule.** Each view has one full-width subject directly under its sentence. Supporting material goes below it in sections, never beside it in a panel wall.

**The Constant Width Rule.** Controls whose label changes reserve the width of their longest possible label, so selecting a value never moves the layout.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat. Depth comes from tonal paper bands (`paper-band`, `paper-deep`) and hairline rules. Shadows exist only on elements that float above the page, and on a linked card under the pointer.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop** (`0 6px 18px rgba(31, 29, 26, 0.14), 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 29, 26, 0.08)`; dark `0 6px 18px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`): menus, tooltips, toasts.
- **Lift** (`0 8px 20px rgba(31, 29, 26, 0.08)`; dark `0 8px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`): a linked card on hover, together with a 2px rise.
- **Flash** (a 6px ring in `flown-wash` that fades over 1.2s): the aircraft card when a lead-sentence phrase points at it.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Page Rule.** Nothing resting on the page casts a shadow. A shadow means "this floats" or "this responds to you".

## Shapes

Gently rounded, never sharp and never bubbly. Cards, menus and the aircraft card use 12px corners; controls, rows, thumbnails, tooltips and icon buttons 8px; toasts 10px; bar-chart tops and focus outlines 4px; the phrase highlight 2px. Pills (999px) are kept for the segmented control and small outlined chips (Review, +1 day). Borders are 1px hairlines; there are no thick strokes outside the data. Photos are cropped to 3:2 in cards and thumbnails and 16:10 on the aircraft card. Icons are Lucide line icons at 1.75 stroke (2.25 for checks), inline SVG, sized to the text.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet and solid; the only filled control is the primary action.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px), 36px high, 14px side padding, 600 weight at 0.9375rem.
- **Primary:** ink fill with paper text (Add). Hover darkens to `button-hover` (lightens in dark theme). Pressing moves it down 1px.
- **Quiet:** transparent with a strong hairline outline and ink text; hover adds the ink wash.
- **Icon button:** 36px square, transparent, `ink-2` icon at 18px; hover adds the ink wash and darkens the icon.
- **Text link with arrow:** 600 weight, no underline at rest, underline on hover.
- **Transitions:** 160ms on background, border and transform with the standard ease.

### Segmented period control
Outlined pill (1px `rule-strong`), 36px segments with 30px side padding, equal-width columns, hairline dividers between segments. The selected segment fills with `paper-deep` and shows a check that scales in from 60% over 180ms; padding is fixed so the check never shifts the label. Recent years are segments; older years collapse into an "Earlier" segment with a chevron that opens a menu, and its label reserves the width of the longest year so the control never changes width. On very narrow screens the whole control becomes a single menu button.

### Menus
Paper background, hairline border, 12px corners, 6px padding, pop shadow, 6px below the trigger. Items are 40px high, 8px corners, with a check column that shows on the current item; hover and focus use the ink wash.

### Lead sentence (signature)
A headline-size sentence in the owner's plain voice with the period's figures inside it at 650. Phrases that refer to data carry a 2px underline in their ink: blue for flown, orange for spotted, half-and-half for both. Hovering or pressing a phrase fills it with that ink's wash and isolates its layer: on Home the map's arcs, spots or the overlap; on Flights that route's arcs and rows; on a flight the page scrolls to the aircraft card and flashes it, because the flight map has no layers.

### Fact list

Stacked stats under the lead sentence, after Flighty's passport page: a label above a large value, a muted detail line below. Order: longest flight (route; distance and flight number), time in the air (hours and minutes; number of flights), most flown airline (number of flights), most flown aircraft (registration; flights and type; shown only when one aircraft was flown more than once), most spotted type (number of aircraft). A stat with no data is left out.

- Label: full noun phrase, 0.8125rem, `ink-3`.
- Value: 1.25rem, weight 650, -0.015em, `ink`, tabular figures. Units step down to 0.72em, weight 500, `ink-2`, so the figure reads first (`141h 15m`). Model names with a hyphen (737-800) do not break at it.
- Detail: 0.875rem, `ink-3`, 2px below the value; parts joined with a middle dot.
- Grid: `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(190px, 1fr))` with 20px by 32px gaps, 32px below the lead sentence. Columns keep their positions when a period has fewer stats. Two columns from 720px down (18px by 20px); a last stat on its own takes the full row.

No rules, icons, colons or cards. Hairlines are kept only where rows are too wide to follow by alignment alone (the flight list, the times strip, the seat and ticket details). A status that needs action (an entry to review) is not a stat: it sits on its own line under the stats, with its icon and a link.

### Maps
- **World map (Home):** Equal Earth projection, full column width, cropped to the period's latitudes with 12° margin. Land in `land` with a paper-coloured coastline, a faint ink graticule. Flown arcs are 1.75px blue great circles at 85% opacity with a 12px invisible hit line; spotting dots are orange with a 2px paper ring. Isolated layers drop others to 12% opacity; an active arc goes to 3px at full opacity.
- **Flight map:** orthographic, centred on the route: recorded track in 2.5px blue over a 6px paper halo; great-circle reference dashed in `ink-3` (or 2px blue dashed when it is the only path); airports as ink dots with a paper ring and 11px 600 labels with a paper stroke.
- **Legend:** above the map, 0.8125rem `ink-2`, with an 18px line swatch for arcs and a 10px dot for spots.

### Cards / Containers
Photo cards borrowed from a gallery: the photo sits flush on top, the caption block below is tinted `paper-band`.
- **Corner Style:** 12px, with the photo clipped to the same corners.
- **Background:** `paper-band` caption block; `paper-deep` behind a loading photo, with a shimmer sweep and the registration as a fallback.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; Lift with a 2px rise on hover when the card is a link.
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 14px 16px 16px (aircraft card 18px 20px). Registration at 600 and 1.0625rem (1.5rem on the aircraft card), type in `ink-2`, then flown and spotted events each with an 8px ink dot.
- **Credit:** every photo carries its photographer credit and a link to the source, 0.75rem in `ink-3`, pinned to the bottom of the caption block.
- **Gallery:** auto-fill grid with 240px minimum columns and 20px by 16px gaps; a scroll-snap strip on phones.

### Flight list rows
A seven-column grid (date, route, flight, aircraft, duration, distance, track) aligned on the **first baseline**, so a two-line cell and a one-line cell share the top line. 14px 12px padding, hairline bottom, 8px corners, `paper-band` on hover or when its route is isolated. Route codes at 600 with a small blue arc arrow; cities below in `ink-3` at 0.8125rem. Numbers right-aligned with tabular figures. Months are grouped under a 650 month heading with a hairline and a muted sum. A row that needs a look carries an outlined pill chip with an info icon and the word "Review".

### Times strip
Four equal cells (boarding, takeoff, landing, duration) between hairlines, with hairline dividers. Each cell: label in `ink-3`, the clock at Clock size, then the airport's local date and UTC offset, plus an outlined "+1 day" pill when the day changes. Two by two on phones.

### Tooltips and toasts
Both are inverted: ink background, paper text, pop shadow. Tooltips are 0.8125rem, 8px 10px padding, 8px corners, max 280px, with muted lines in `tip-muted`; they rise 4px into place over 120ms and never take the pointer. Toasts sit centred 28px above the bottom edge, 10px 16px padding, 10px corners, rising 12px over 180ms; they announce politely to assistive technology.

### Status lines
A row of icon plus sentence at 0.9375rem in `ink-2`, the icon nudged 3px down to sit on the first line. Passed checks use a circle-check icon in `good`; a finding uses an info icon with the text in ink and a bold lead word ("Needs a look."). Import provenance uses the info icon in the default tone.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 38px high, 1px `rule-strong` outline, 8px corners, `field` background, 12px padding (36px on the left when a search icon sits inside), 0.9375rem.
- **Hover:** outline darkens to `rule-hover`.
- **Focus:** outline turns flown blue with a 3px `flown-wash` ring; no browser outline.
- **Checkboxes:** native, 16px, accent in flown blue.

### Navigation
- **Header band:** sticky, `paper-band` with a hairline bottom, 64px high (56px on phones): wordmark, nav links, then sync state, theme button and Add on the right.
- **Desktop links:** 0.9375rem `ink-2`, 6px 12px padding, 8px corners; hover adds the ink wash; the current page is ink, 600 weight, on the selected wash. Unavailable sections stay visible in `ink-3` and do nothing on hover.
- **Phone tab bar:** fixed to the bottom, `paper-band` with a hairline top, icons at 22px above 0.6875rem labels, minimum 56px per tab, safe-area padding below. The current tab turns `flown-ink` with no fill. The bar's colour continues below the screen edge so the page never shows through while the browser toolbar moves.

### Motion
One easing, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, for every transition. Durations: 120ms tooltips, 140-160ms hover and colour changes, 180ms segments, phrases and toasts, 200ms map layers and card lift, 260ms photo fade-in, 1.2s card flash, 1.4s linear shimmer. Reduced-motion users get effectively instant transitions.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep `paper` (#FAF8F1) as the light ground and `paper-dark` as the dark ground, and support both themes, following the device unless the user picks one.
- **Do** colour flight data blue and spotting data orange, using each theme's validated pair, and split the two for data that is both.
- **Do** open each view with one plain sentence carrying its figures, and underline data phrases 2px in their ink so they point at the map layer or the aircraft.
- **Do** show every precise time with its date and UTC offset, in 24-hour airport-local time.
- **Do** use tabular figures for every number that aligns or updates.
- **Do** give every status an icon and a word.
- **Do** credit every aircraft photo with the photographer and a link to the source.
- **Do** reserve the longest label's width in controls whose label changes.
- **Do** align multi-line list rows on their first baseline.
- **Do** separate sections with hairlines and space, and use `paper-band` for raised areas.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a serif, a webfont or a monospace face; the system sans covers every role.
- **Don't** add a third data colour or use the data inks as decoration; blue outside data is limited to focus, caret, selection and the current phone tab.
- **Don't** build KPI tiles or a wall of side panels; a figure belongs in a sentence or a chart.
- **Don't** show a clock time without its date, or a status by colour alone.
- **Don't** put shadows on anything resting on the page.
- **Don't** add small uppercase labels above headings; the wordmark is the only tracked capitals in the interface.
